Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 9, 2024

1. Information We Collect

FriendsChat collects the following information:

  • Personal Information: First name, last name, email address, student ID code, username
  • Student Data: Grade level, academic year information
  • Usage Data: Messages (encrypted), server membership, channel activity
  • Device Information: MAC address (for ban enforcement)
  • Account Data: Login timestamps, approval status, role assignments

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide and maintain the Service
  • Verify user identity and prevent unauthorized access
  • Monitor compliance with Terms of Service
  • Ensure a safe and appropriate environment for all users
  • Process teacher access requests for student data
  • Enforce bans and prevent ban evasion
  • Automatically update grade levels annually

3. Message Encryption and Access

Important Privacy Notice:

  • All messages are encrypted using industry-standard encryption
  • The master administrator (Ruben Sutton, username: SuperCode111) has the ability to decrypt and view ALL messages for moderation purposes
  • Regular administrators have limited access to messages only when necessary for moderation
  • There is NO expectation of complete privacy in communications on this platform
  • Messages may be reviewed at any time for safety and compliance purposes

4. Data Sharing

We may share your information:

  • With authorized administrators for moderation purposes
  • With teachers who have received proper authorization through the teacher access request system
  • When required by law or valid legal process
  • To protect the rights and safety of users

We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for the following periods:

  • Account Information: Indefinitely while your account is active
  • Messages: Indefinitely for moderation and educational purposes
  • Student Data: Updated annually and retained throughout enrollment
  • Ban Records: Permanent, including MAC addresses

6. Student Privacy

Special protections for student data:

  • Student information is stored securely and access is restricted
  • Grade levels are automatically updated annually
  • Teacher access to student data requires multi-level approval
  • All access to student data is logged and auditable

7. Security Measures

We implement security measures including:

  • Password hashing using industry-standard algorithms
  • Message encryption
  • Secure database connections
  • JWT-based authentication
  • MAC address tracking for ban enforcement

8. Admin Confidentiality and Impartiality Agreement

All administrators are bound by a confidentiality agreement:

  • Administrators must not misuse their access to spy on users
  • Access to messages should only be for legitimate moderation purposes
  • Administrators must maintain impartiality in moderation decisions
  • Administrators must not share accessed information inappropriately
  • Violations of this agreement may result in removal of admin privileges

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request information about the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account (subject to retention requirements)
  • Report privacy concerns to administrators

10. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal tracking technologies:

  • Authentication tokens (JWT) for login sessions
  • No third-party tracking cookies
  • Session data for maintaining login state

11. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be communicated to users. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact Information

For privacy concerns or questions, contact the master administrator at: ruben.sutton@school.edu
