ARTICLE 1: ADMINISTRATOR RESPONSIBILITIES

1.1 Primary Duties

As a FriendsChat Administrator, you agree to:

  • Monitor chat activity for rule violations
  • Respond to user reports in a timely manner (within 24 hours)
  • Issue appropriate warnings and punishments according to platform guidelines
  • Maintain accurate records of all moderation actions
  • Participate in administrator training and meetings
  • Uphold community standards and platform values

ARTICLE 2: CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS

2.1 User Privacy

I AGREE to maintain strict confidentiality regarding all user data, including but not limited to: personal information, private messages, IP addresses, email addresses, and any other identifying information accessed in my capacity as an administrator.

2.2 Platform Security

I AGREE not to disclose any platform vulnerabilities, security measures, admin tools, or internal processes to unauthorized individuals.

2.3 Administrator Discussions

I AGREE that all discussions in administrator-only channels are confidential and shall not be shared with non-administrators.

ARTICLE 3: ABUSE OF POWER PROHIBITIONS

3.1 Unauthorized Access

I AGREE not to access user accounts, private conversations, or system features beyond what is necessary for legitimate moderation purposes.

3.2 Personal Gain

I AGREE not to use administrator privileges for personal gain, favoritism, or to gain unfair advantages in any platform activities.

3.3 Retaliation

I AGREE not to retaliate against users who report me or disagree with my moderation decisions.

3.4 Power Misuse

I AGREE not to issue punishments without valid reason, manipulate evidence, or abuse any administrator tools or features.

ARTICLE 4: UNBIASED ENFORCEMENT

4.1 Equal Treatment

I AGREE to enforce all platform rules equally and fairly, without discrimination based on personal relationships, race, gender, religion, nationality, or any other characteristic.

4.2 Conflict of Interest

I AGREE to recuse myself from moderation decisions involving close friends, family members, or individuals with whom I have a personal conflict.

4.3 Evidence-Based Decisions

I AGREE to base all moderation decisions on evidence and platform rules, not on personal opinions or biases.

ARTICLE 5: DOCUMENTATION AND ACCOUNTABILITY

5.1 Accurate Records

I AGREE to maintain accurate and complete records of all moderation actions, including warnings, mutes, bans, and Direct Warnings.

5.2 Form Completion

I AGREE to complete and print all required moderation forms for actions including slowmode, mutes, and bans, and to sign these forms personally (not digitally).

5.3 Master Admin Approval

I AGREE to obtain master administrator approval via physical signature for all temporary mutes, permanent mutes, temporary bans, and permanent bans before they take effect.

ARTICLE 6: DATA HANDLING

6.1 Secure Handling

I AGREE to handle all sensitive information securely and never download, screenshot, or store user data on personal devices without authorization.

6.2 Physical Documents

I AGREE to file all completed moderation forms securely and surrender them to the master administrator for secure storage.

ARTICLE 7: CONDUCT STANDARDS

7.1 Professional Behavior

I AGREE to maintain professional and respectful conduct at all times while performing administrator duties.

7.2 Rule Compliance

I AGREE to comply with all platform rules and policies, and to set a positive example for the community.

7.3 Communication Standards

I AGREE to communicate clearly and respectfully with users, even in difficult situations.

ARTICLE 8: COOPERATION AND TRANSPARENCY

8.1 Investigation Cooperation

I AGREE to cooperate fully with any investigations into my conduct or moderation decisions, including providing evidence and testimony as requested.

8.2 Incident Reporting

I AGREE to immediately report any serious incidents, security breaches, or violations of this agreement to the master administrator.

ARTICLE 9: TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT

9.1 Initial Training

I AGREE to complete all required administrator training within 7 days of appointment.

9.2 Ongoing Education

I AGREE to participate in ongoing training sessions and stay updated on platform policy changes.

ARTICLE 10: RESIGNATION AND TERMINATION

10.1 Resignation Notice

I AGREE to provide at least 2 weeks notice if I choose to resign from my administrator position, except in emergency circumstances.

10.2 Return of Materials

I AGREE to return all administrator materials and delete any saved information upon resignation or termination.

10.3 Post-Termination Confidentiality

I AGREE that all confidentiality obligations continue indefinitely after my administrator role ends.

ARTICLE 11: CONSEQUENCES OF VIOLATION

I understand that violation of any clause in this agreement may result in:

  • Immediate termination of administrator privileges
  • Permanent ban from the platform
  • Reporting to school authorities
  • Legal action for damages or breach of confidentiality
  • Notification to law enforcement if criminal activity is suspected
